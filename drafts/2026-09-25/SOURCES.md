## Behind the Scenes — the Elf burp
- ScreenRant, HuffPost UK, IMDb News, SlashFilm — the belch in Elf (2003)
  was dubbed by voice actor Maurice LaMarche (The Brain in Pinky and the
  Brain, Kif in Futurama), not performed vocally by Will Ferrell.
- LaMarche has confirmed this himself in interviews including on The
  Ship-It Show podcast and with People TV (per SlashFilm's own reporting
  on those interviews), including the detail about the size of his first
  residual check.
- UNCSA (uncsa.edu) — Wade Wilson, Elf's actual head sound designer and
  now Sound Design faculty at the University of North Carolina School of
  the Arts, discussed how the scene's sound was built in post-production.
  Note: the UNCSA page itself is a short news write-up introducing a
  longer student interview; it confirms Wilson's role and that he
  discussed the scene's construction, but the specific technical details
  of that conversation weren't accessible from the page text alone — the
  copy stays to what's confirmed (that it was layered/shaped in post) and
  doesn't invent specifics from the interview itself.

## Improvised or Scripted? — Jaws
- The Hollywood Reporter — screenwriter Carl Gottlieb's account of "You're
  gonna need a bigger boat" starting as a real crew in-joke about the
  production's stingy budget, written into the script during rewrites.
- Top10Films.co.uk, summarizing Roy Scheider's own 2000 interview
  (syndicated by the San Francisco Chronicle) — Scheider saying he wasn't
  fully sure himself whether his delivery was scripted or ad-libbed in the
  moment, despite the line appearing in the shooting draft. Verdict: BOTH.
