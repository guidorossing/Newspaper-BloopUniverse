## The Front Page — Spider-Man's all-time domestic record
- Variety, Deadline, The Hollywood Reporter, TheWrap, Forbes — all Sept.
  16, 2026 — "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" crossed $936.773M domestically,
  surpassing Star Wars: The Force Awakens's $936.6M (held since 2015) as
  the highest-grossing film ever at the North American box office, 47
  days after release; worldwide total $2.45B; fastest film to reach every
  domestic milestone from $400M through $900M.
- Variety — "Sony's Tom Rothman on 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' Blockbuster
  Success" (Lumiere Summit remarks) — "miraculous," and the
  pandemic/strikes quote used as the pull-quote, printed verbatim.
- The "no gag reel yet" framing is this paper's own observation, not a
  claim attributed to any outlet — Brand New Day is still in theatrical
  release, so no home-video gag reel exists yet, unlike No Way Home's
  (released March 2022, covered in Vol. 1, No. 1).

## Improvised or Scripted? — Being John Malkovich
- Charlie Kaufman's published shooting script (dailyscript.com) — the
  beer-can beat is written in the script almost exactly as filmed: a
  motorist shouts "Hey, Malkovich! Think fast!" before throwing a can
  that hits him.
- This directly contradicts the "ad-libbed by a drunk extra, who got a
  pay raise" version that circulates widely online (found via multiple
  WebSearch results) — I could not find a single named, dated primary
  source for the pay-raise claim, so the edition explicitly declines to
  print it as fact while noting it's the popular version of the story.
  Verdict is SCRIPTED, sourced to the primary document (the script)
  rather than to a person's recollection.

approving.

- **Practical effect on Fan Corner:** I could not pull any comment this
  session, fresh or otherwise, without fabricating it. The section

  Guido asked for this fixed differently, since Video of the Week

  (four videos currently being cut) and asked Fan Corner run a preview of
  what's coming instead of a comment. Fan Corner now says three of those
