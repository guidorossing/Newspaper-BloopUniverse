Behind the Bloop · The story behind the laughter

"A Friend From Work": How Thor's Funniest Line Came From A Very Special Visitor

It's one of the biggest laughs in Thor: Ragnarok — Thor spots the Hulk in the arena and beams: "He's a friend from work!" Pure improv genius from Chris Hemsworth? Not quite.

As Hemsworth has told it, the line was suggested by a young Make-A-Wish visitor on set that day. The kid pitched it, Hemsworth said it, the crew lost it — and the line stayed in the final cut, becoming one of the most-quoted moments in the entire MCU.

Proof that the best "mistakes" in Hollywood sometimes aren't mistakes at all — they're gifts.
