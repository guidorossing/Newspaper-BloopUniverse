Questions from the mailroom

Good questions. Honest answers.

How do I receive the paper?

Straight to your inbox every Friday morning. No app, no login, no actual trees harmed.

What do I get for free?

One complete edition, sent the moment you sign up — the same paper premium readers get, start to finish. It's a taster rather than a weekly subscription: the Friday editions are for Insiders.

Why isn't the weekly paper free?

Because it takes a full day a week to research, write and lay out. $9.99 a month keeps it independent, ad-free and worth reading — and you can cancel in two clicks, whenever you like.

Is any of it made up?

No. Every claim in the paper is traceable to a source — a trade report, a commentary track, a printed interview, or the gag reel itself. Where a story is famous but unconfirmed, we say so in the text. When we get something wrong, we print the correction the following Friday.

Can I unsubscribe?

Any time, with one click at the bottom of every email. No hard feelings. Well, some.

Who makes this?

The same people behind the BloopUniverse YouTube channel — your front-row seat to the moments behind the magic.

Where does the premium money go?

Into making more bloopers videos and better editions. And, occasionally, coffee.
