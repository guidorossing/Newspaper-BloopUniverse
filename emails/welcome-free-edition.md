### BEHIND THE BLOOP · The story behind the laughter

## "A Friend From Work"

It's one of the biggest laughs in *Thor: Ragnarok*. Thor spots the Hulk across the arena, his face lights up, and he beams: **"He's a friend from work!"**

Pure improv genius from Chris Hemsworth? Not quite.

As Hemsworth has told it, the line came from a young Make-A-Wish visitor who was on set that day. The kid pitched it. Hemsworth tried it. The crew lost it — and the line stayed in the final cut, becoming one of the most-quoted moments in the entire MCU.

Proof that the best "mistakes" in Hollywood sometimes aren't mistakes at all. They're gifts.
