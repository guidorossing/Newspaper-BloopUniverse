Indiana Jones Didn't Win That Fight. He Had Dysentery.
The biggest laugh in Raiders of the Lost Ark exists because its star could not stand up for very long.

Steven Spielberg wanted a duel. Not a scuffle — a proper one, whip against sword, the definitive version of a thing adventure films had been doing badly for fifty years. He had searched for a swordsman good enough to carry it and found one in Terry Richards. The scene was planned, choreographed and ready to shoot in Tunisia.

Then Harrison Ford got dysentery.

He was, by every account of that shoot, extremely unwell. Standing up in desert heat for the hours an elaborate fight sequence demands was not on the table. So Ford made a suggestion.

  "Let's just shoot the son of a bitch."

Spielberg's reply, as he has told it since: I was thinking that as well.

So Indy watches the swordsman perform his whole flourishing routine, looks tired, and shoots him. It is over in four seconds. It is the single biggest laugh in the film, and it works precisely because the audience has been set up to expect the duel that Spielberg had actually planned to give them.

It also put the production a day and a half ahead of schedule, which is the sort of detail that gets a director to say yes very quickly.

This is the thing we keep finding. A frightening number of the moments people quote back to each other were not designed. They were what happened when the plan collapsed and somebody in the room was quick enough to catch what fell out of it.

Told by Harrison Ford and Steven Spielberg in interviews over the years, and recounted again by the film's producers in a Hollywood Reporter roundtable.
