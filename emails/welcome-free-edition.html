Takes So Bad, The Cameras Had To Go Back In
The Marvel bloopers that didn't just break the cast — they broke the shooting schedule.
By The Bloop Times Editorial Desk

There are bloopers you laugh at, and there are bloopers that cost a studio money.

This week's investigation is about the second kind: takes so thoroughly demolished by giggling superheroes that the only fix was to shoot the entire scene again. Heroes forgetting lines they'd written on their own hands. Props betraying their owners at the worst possible second. At least one Avenger laughing so hard the take was declared legally unusable.

Marvel's editors cut these moments for a reason. We put them back for a better one.

"The scene was unusable. The take was priceless."

Because what makes a blooper worth saving isn't the mistake itself — it's the half-second afterwards, when a multi-million-dollar superhero remembers they're a person.

That's the moment the studio cuts. That's the moment we print.

Behind the Bloop · The story behind the laughter
"A Friend From Work"

It's one of the biggest laughs in Thor: Ragnarok. Thor spots the Hulk across the arena, his face lights up, and he beams: "He's a friend from work!"

Pure improv genius from Chris Hemsworth? Not quite.

As Hemsworth has told it, the line came from a young Make-A-Wish visitor who was on set that day. The kid pitched it. Hemsworth tried it. The crew lost it — and the line stayed in the final cut, becoming one of the most-quoted moments in the entire MCU.

Proof that the best "mistakes" in Hollywood sometimes aren't mistakes at all. They're gifts.
